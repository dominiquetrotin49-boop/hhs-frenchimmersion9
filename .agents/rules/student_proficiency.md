# Student Proficiency & Target Audience Profile

## 🎓 Target Student Level:
- **Proficiency Level**: **Intermediate-Mid** (ACTFL scale / ~B1.1 CEFR)
- **Program & Grade**: **9th Grade French Immersion (9e année immersion française)**
- **Primary Guides**: Jasmine & Kader (14-year-old high school teenagers)

## 📌 Pedagogical Guidelines for Activities & Content:

1. **Language Complexity**:
   - Use natural, authentic French suitable for Intermediate-Mid immersion students.
   - Target grammar targets include: Passé composé vs. Imparfait (storytelling), Subjonctif (advice & necessity), Conditionnel (hypothetical situations), Pronoms démonstratifs & connecteurs logiques (argumentative writing).

2. **Scaffolding & Support**:
   - Provide rich, interactive word banks (Boîte à mots), targeted vocabulary chips, and English translations (`Anglais:`) on the back of 3D flashcards.
   - Avoid over-simplifying prompts to beginner level; allow students to write detailed paragraphs, describe complex hero archetypes, and express opinions.

3. **Activities & Games**:
   - Design activities (3D flashcards, character analysis studios, pronunciation games, quizzes) tailored for 14-year-old Grade 9 immersion learners.
